# Rebrand Brief — Precision Builders (Q3, after lots close)

## Why now (and not now)
The refresh is real and worth doing — but it runs **after** Copper Creek closes out,
ahead of the Cottonwood Canyon / 407 launch. Do **not** put a half-formed rebrand on the
time-sensitive July mailers; a half-baked identity on a time-sensitive piece is the worst
of both worlds. Copper Creek is the bridge: close lots, bank testimonials and finished-home
photography, then relaunch the identity into the next big project.

## The two-brand architecture

| | **Copper Creek Estates** | **Precision Builders** |
|---|---|---|
| Sells | The dream / the place | The proof it gets built right |
| Feels | Premium, elegant, peaceful, spacious | Strong, capable, tough, hands-on, high-trust |
| Faces | Buyers, lifestyle, MLS, finished homes | Job sites, founder, process, crews |
| Content | Polished — homes, kitchens, land, sunsets, golf | Raw — POV walkthroughs, crews, details |

They **share DNA, not skins.** Linked, clearly related, never identical.

> **First-principles view:** Copper Creek sells the dream. Precision Builders proves the
> dream can actually be built.

## Visual system (starting direction)
- **Palette:** black base + high contrast. **Copper** (ties to Copper Canyon/Creek) as the
  signature, **red** as the aggressive accent. Black + copper + red.
- **Copper Creek** leans the elegant end of the system; **Precision job-site** leans
  hardcore — he's explicitly fine with skull-and-crossbones "don't trash my site" energy on
  field signage.
- **Logo:** retire the self-made spray-paint-roof mark (wife "likes it, doesn't love it").
  Watch letter-spacing (the "P/E too close" issue he flagged on the test font).
- **Road test everything.** His #1 brand touchpoint is *driving by*; #2 is *MLS resales*.
  Signs must read instantly at 45 mph. Design for the road first, the business card second.

## Process decision (client's call)
Run a ~$300 logo-contest pass for raw volume, **or** have Local Bzz develop tight concepts.

**Recommendation: Local Bzz concepts.** A contest gives 200 generic marks with no strategy
behind the two-brand split. Use a contest only for quick visual stimulus if he wants options
to react to.

## Deliverables when greenlit
- Logo system (both brands)
- Color / type spec
- Sign templates (road-legibility proofed)
- Job-site signage suite
- Social templates for each brand's content lane
