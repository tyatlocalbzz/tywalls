# Event One-Pager

## Copper Creek Estates — Sunset Lot Tour & Golf Cart Preview

**The idea:** Not a formal open house — a casual, premium community evening. Warm
prospects arrive on their own time, feel the lifestyle, see the carts, and tour
available lots until they talk themselves into the next step.

## Format
- **Where:** On-site, Copper Creek Estates (tent — it's outdoor/county)
- **When:** Saturday evening, ~6:30–8:30 PM, sunset timing. **Not July 4th** (county, no
  fireworks). Pick the Saturday before or after.
- **Vibe:** Come-and-go, no schedule. Arrive, mingle, leave whenever.

## On site
- Tent, drinks, light food/snacks, music
- 2–3 demo golf carts + the Kawasaki Mule for tours
- Guides who know the lots: him, his wife, oldest daughter
- Self-tour option: "Grab a cart, go drive the lots"
- Partner tables: title company, lender, real estate, golf-cart vendor
- Precision Builders signage / branded carts

## The hook → the capture
- **Golf-cart giveaway raffle — must be present / must enter to win.** This *is* the lead
  capture: QR → name, phone, email → entry. Not a fishbowl of paper.
- Announce winner near the end to hold the room.

## Promotion
- Direct mail (event details on the July drop if date is locked)
- Facebook + Instagram, organic + boosted, with shares from partners
- Text/email to all warm leads from the last mailer
- Realtor / title / lender cross-promotion

## Possible names
- *Copper Creek Sunset Lot Tour*
- *Custom Homes, Golf Carts & Sunset Tours*
- *Build Your Next Chapter — Copper Creek Preview*

## Open decisions for client
- Exact date
- Whether the raffle cart is the demo unit he's already buying
- Final partner list
