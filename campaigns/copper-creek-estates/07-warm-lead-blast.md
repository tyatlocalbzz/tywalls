# Warm-Lead Text & Email Blast

The cheapest, fastest conversions available right now: people who already raised a hand on
the last mailer. Goal — re-open the conversation and route them to a tour or the event.

**Audience:** everyone who called, replied, or inquired after the previous drop, plus past
tour-takers who didn't buy. Segment lightly: (A) inquired-never-toured, (B) toured-never-bought.

**Compliance:** texts only to people who opted in or initiated contact. Every text ends with
opt-out. Send during daylight hours, weekdays/Saturday. Keep it personal, not blasty.

---

## SMS

### Text 1 — Inquired, never toured (Segment A)
> Hey [First] — it's [Name] with Precision Builders / Copper Creek. We've got a few one-acre
> lots left and a July offer worth a look (custom golf cart with a lot purchase). Want me to
> set up a quick golf-cart tour this week? Reply STOP to opt out.

### Text 2 — Toured, never bought (Segment B)
> [First], it's [Name] at Copper Creek. The lots you looked at are getting thin and we're
> running a July golf-cart offer. Worth a second look before they're gone? Happy to walk you
> through what's left. Reply STOP to opt out.

### Text 3 — Event invite (both segments, once date is set)
> [First] — we're doing a Sunset Lot Tour at Copper Creek on [date]. Drinks, music, golf
> carts, and the last one-acre lots at golden hour. Come drive a cart and see them. RSVP
> here: [link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

## Cadence
1. **Day 0** — Email 1/2 by segment
2. **Day 2** — Text 1/2 to non-openers / non-responders
3. **Event window** — Email 3 + Text 3, ~10 days then ~2 days before the date
4. **Follow up every lead reply within one business day** — that's the whole point

## Notes
- Personalize the first line with what they actually told you (lot #, what they liked).
- Keep tax language soft until real Denton County numbers are confirmed.
- Hold golf-cart specifics loose until the offer mechanics are locked.
