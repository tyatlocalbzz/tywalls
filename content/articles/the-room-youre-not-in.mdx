Your best customers are already saying the exact words that would make your next ten sales easier. You just aren’t in the room when they say them.

I’ve sat in that room hundreds of times. The owner is usually surprised by at least one thing that comes back. Not because the customers are hiding anything. Because nobody ever asked the question in a way that made the answer safe to give.

The language your top clients use when they explain why they chose you is almost never the language you use on your website or in your proposals. That gap is expensive. It’s the difference between sounding like every other option and sounding like the only reasonable one.

I don’t go in looking for compliments. I go in looking for the specific, unprompted reasons someone felt confident enough to write the check. Those reasons are the only marketing that actually works when you’re not in the room.

The work is simple once you hear it. You stop guessing what to say and start using the words that already made someone trust you.
