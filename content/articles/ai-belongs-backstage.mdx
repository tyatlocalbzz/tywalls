---
title: "AI belongs backstage"
date: "2026-04-28"
excerpt: "The moment AI shows up in customer-facing content, every other piece of work gets doubted. Use it in the kitchen. Never on the plate."
---

Most businesses I talk to are feeding AI the same generic inputs everyone else is using and then wondering why the output feels like everyone else’s.

AI is infrastructure. It lowers the cost of doing good work. The second it appears on the plate, the customer starts wondering what else was automated. Trust evaporates fast once people feel they’ve been handed something that wasn’t made for them.

I use it heavily. Transcription, first-pass research, organizing notes, drafting structures that I then tear apart and rewrite in a real voice. The customer never sees the scaffolding.

If your best customer sat down and read what you just published, would they recognize the person they actually bought from? If the answer is no, the AI was on the plate.

The businesses that will still matter in five years are the ones whose customers can hear their actual voice when they’re not in the room. AI can help you get there faster. It cannot replace the voice.
